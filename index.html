Drive

FWDBACKLR

fwd +0 · turn +0 → 00 00

Drag the knob in any direction: up/down is speed, sideways is turn, diagonals curve. Let go to stop. Keyboard: WASD or arrows (combine for curves), Space to stop.

Speed mode treats FED0 as a joystick: byte 0 = forward/back, byte 1 = turn, both signed −127…127. This is a hypothesis from the sniffed values, so start low.

Head & light

The slider sends any value 0–255 to FED1. Only 00, 87 and FF are confirmed — the in-between values are yours to map.

Eyes · camera vision & face

Camera off

How it works: LOOI learns what the floor looks like from the patch right in front of it, then checks each of 9 columns for how far up the image the floor reaches. Tall green bars mean open space, short red bars mean something is in the way (an obstacle, or the desk edge). The distance and cliff sensors still have the final say. Use the ultra-wide rear camera if it's listed, and set the field of view to about 110°.

Face mode fills the screen with LOOI's eyes, sized for the cover screen. The eyes look where LOOI is heading, react to obstacles, and blink. On the face: press and hold to start or stop exploring, double-tap to exit. If the page is hidden while folding, the camera and Bluetooth reconnect on their own when it's visible again.

Training wheels · learn my driving

No style learned
  1. Record yourself driving with the joystick for 1–3 minutes. Cruise around, and steer away from obstacles the way you like: early or late, arcing or stopping to turn, left or right.
  2. Learn my style turns the recording into a driving profile.
  3. Drive like me runs that profile on its own. The obstacle stop distance, edge escape and lift detection from Explore always apply on top, and grabbing the joystick hands control back to you.

Position is estimated from the drive commands (there are no wheel sensors), so it drifts over time. Calibrate it first: run the speed test, measure how far LOOI moved in mm, and enter that as "mm/s at speed 100" in the Eyes panel. Camera ranging learns by itself whenever the distance sensor sees something; drive toward a few objects to teach it.

AI pilot · OpenAI

AI idle

Autonomous mode lets LOOI decide on its own every 10 seconds, for at most 5 minutes. Every move still goes through the safety checks. STOP cancels everything.

Connect LOOI, paste your OpenAI key, then say hi. Try "come towards me slowly", "look up and turn on your light" or "what's your battery?"
